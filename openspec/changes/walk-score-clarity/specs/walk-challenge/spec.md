## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Visible weight
The "Italia a piedi" row SHALL show its weight as a badge ("vale N"), locked or unlocked, and the achievements header SHALL say that Italia a piedi counts N in the total. N MUST be greater than 10 (proposed 25).

#### Scenario: Badge and total
- **WHEN** the achievements tab is opened
- **THEN** the Italia a piedi row shows "vale N", and the header total equals the number of other achievements plus N
